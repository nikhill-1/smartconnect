ACREDITION & AFFILIATIONS

ORGINS

In loving memory of the late Mr. Teegala Krupakar Reddy, the
esteemed founder of Teegala Ram Reddy Educational Society,
whose legacy of self-reliance and educational enlightenment
continues to inspire. With a distinguished career spanning over
two decades in teaching and industry.

His astute leadership steered the institution towards remarkable
heights, fostering an ethos of academic excellence and holistic
development. Through his enduring dedication, Mr. Teegala
Krupakar Reddy's legacy lives on, shaping the TRR Group
of Institutions as a beacon of transformative education and
honorably empowering generations of students

CHAIRMAN

In homage to the vibrant leadership of Mr. Teegala Nitesh Reddy, the dynamic Chairman
of Teegala Ram Reddy Educational Society, whose fervor and vigilance have guided the
institution since its inception in 2004. A visionary pioneer, he initiated Hyderabad's first
private Polytechnic, laying the foundation for excellence. His tireless dedication expanded
further with the establishment of the B. Pharmacy college in 2006 and the establishment of
Engineering Technology College in 2023, fueled by a steadfast commitment to quality
education. Today, as Chairman, his illustrious leadership continues to elevate the
institution, fulfilling his vision of enlightenment and empowerment through education. His
remarkable contributions adorn the TRR Educational Society with enduring pride and
distinction.

Under his visionary stewardship, TRR Group of Institutions has flourished into a beacon of
educational excellence, enriching the lives of countless students. Mr. Nitesh Reddy's
unwavering commitment to fostering a culture of academic rigor and holistic development
has propelled the institution to new heights of success, solidifying its reputation as a hub of
innovation and learning. His legacy as Chairman is not only one of remarkable
achievement but also of profound impact, as he continues to inspire generations to strive
for excellence and pursue their dreams.

SECRETARY

Ms. Teegala Mona Reddy plays a pivotal role as a key member of the management team,
dedicated to fostering excellence in education at Teegala Ram Reddy Group of Institutions.
Trained in Teaching Methods and Technology, her expertise underscores her commitment to
advancing educational practices within the institution. With a keen focus on continuous
improvement, Ms. Reddy endeavors to leverage her extensive experience to enhance the
academic experience for both students and faculty alike.

In addition to her contributions within the college, Ms. Reddy's philanthropic endeavors
exemplify her dedication to broader community engagement. As the driving force behind
the establishment of "Rakshitha," an NGO aimed at extending educational opportunities
to underprivileged children in rural Telangana, she demonstrates a deep-seated commitment
to social impact. Her passion for improving educational standards is further evidenced by
her efforts to create a conducive learning environment for pharmacy students, facilitating
their pursuit of successful careers. Ms. Reddy's multifaceted contributions embody the ethos
of TRR Group of Institutions, reflecting a steadfast dedication to educational excellence and
societal welfare.
